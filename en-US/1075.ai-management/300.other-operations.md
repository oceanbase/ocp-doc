# Manage an AI intelligent conversation

<main id="notice" type='notice'>
<h4>Applicability</h4>
<p>This topic applies only to OCP Enterprise Edition. OCP Community Edition does not provide this feature. </p>
</main>

You can manage an AI intelligent conversation as follows.

* **Switch the model**: On the AI intelligent conversation page, click the model in the top navigation bar and select another model from the drop-down list.

  * If the required vendor type is not available in the vendor list, you can click [Add Vendor](../1600.system-management-features/450.manage-external-integration/100.manage-ai-models/200.add-a-model.md) to add it.
  * The switched model is applicable only to the current conversation.

* **Open a new conversation**: On the AI intelligent conversation page, click ![1](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/436/1456.png) in the top navigation bar to open a new conversation, which will overwrite the previous one.

* **View historical conversations**: On the AI intelligent conversation page, click ![1](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/436/1449.png) in the top navigation bar to view historical conversations.

* **Switch to window mode**: On the AI intelligent conversation page, click ![1](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/436/1453.png) in the top navigation bar to switch to side window mode or floating window mode.

* **Minimize the conversation**: On the AI intelligent conversation page, click ![1](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/436/1454.png) in the top navigation bar to close the conversation. After the conversation is closed, you can view the conversation content on the historical conversations page.

* **Modify the retention period of a conversation**: If no conversation is ongoing for a long time, the conversation is retained only for 1 hour. After 1 hour, a new conversation window is opened. You can modify the retention period of a conversation by changing the system parameter `ocp.ai.assistant.session.retention.timeout-seconds`. For more information, see [Modify a system parameter](../1600.system-management-features/500.manage-system-parameter/200.modify-system-parameters.md).
