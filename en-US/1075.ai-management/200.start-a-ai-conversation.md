# Enable AI-powered intelligent conversation

<main id="notice" type='notice'>
<h4>Applicability</h4>
<p>This topic applies only to OCP Enterprise Edition. OCP Community Edition does not support this feature. </p>
</main>

You can refer to this topic to enable AI-powered intelligent conversation. The AI assistant will provide in-depth thinking, analysis, and diagnostics for your questions.

## Prerequisites

* You have [connected a model vendor](../1600.system-management-features/450.manage-external-integration/100.manage-ai-models/100.link-a-model.md) or [added a custom model](../1600.system-management-features/450.manage-external-integration/100.manage-ai-models/200.add-a-model.md).
* You have [set a system model](../1600.system-management-features/450.manage-external-integration/100.manage-ai-models/300.set-system-model.md).

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. On any page of the OCP console, click the AI assistant icon on the right.

3. On the AI assistant page, select **AI Chat** mode.

4. Click **Start Chat**.

5. In the dialog box, enter any questions about OceanBase Database. The AI assistant will provide answers based on the large language model.
