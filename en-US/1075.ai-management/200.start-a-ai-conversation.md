# Enable AI intelligent dialogue

This topic describes how to enable AI intelligent dialogue. The AI assistant will provide in-depth thinking, analysis, and diagnostics for your questions.

## Prerequisites

* You have [connected to a model vendor](../1600.system-management-features/450.manage-external-integration/100.manage-ai-models/100.link-a-model.md) or have [added a custom model](../1600.system-management-features/450.manage-external-integration/100.manage-ai-models/200.add-a-model.md).
* You have [set a system model](../1600.system-management-features/450.manage-external-integration/100.manage-ai-models/300.set-system-model.md).

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. On any page of the OCP console, click the AI assistant icon on the right.

3. On the AI assistant page, select **Intelligent Dialogue** mode.

4. Click **Enable AI Dialogue**.

5. In the dialog box, enter any questions about OceanBase Database. The AI assistant will generate relevant answers based on the large language model.
