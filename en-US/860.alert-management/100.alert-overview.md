# Overview

This topic describes how OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) monitors alerts and sends notifications when an error is detected in an OceanBase cluster or application.

## Introduction

OCP provides alerting and monitoring services for OceanBase clusters, tenants, and their resources so that you can detect business operation failures and resource shortages as soon as possible.

## Alert events

On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Alarm.AlarmEvent">Alert Events</UI-TERM>** tab, you can search for alert events by **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.alarm.Search.Application">Application</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Subscriptions.ObjectType">Object Type</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.alarm.ExpressionFormList.MultipleExpressionFormList.AlarmLevel">Alert Level</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Subscriptions.Keywords">Keyword</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.TaskBubble.StartTime">Start Time</UI-TERM>**, and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.alarm.Search.EndTime">End Time</UI-TERM>**.

Alert events are classified into active alerts and historical alerts.

* Active alerts: alerts in the `Alerting` or `Blocked` state.
* Historical alerts: alerts in the `Cleared`, `Expired`, or `Resolved` state.

In the alert event list, you can view information about alerts in columns such as: **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Event.AlarmDescription">Alert Description</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Information.Detail.CollectDetails.GenerationTime">Generated At</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.CardAbnormalTable.RecoveryTime">Recovery Time</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Subscriptions.New.AlarmClassification">Alert Type</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Information.Detail.CollectDetails.AlertStatus">Alert Status</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Rule.New.AlarmLevel">Alert Level</UI-TERM>**, and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Event.MessagePush">Message Push</UI-TERM>**. You can sort alerts by **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Event.Detail.GenerationTime">Generated At</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.CardAbnormalTable.RecoveryTime">Recovery Time</UI-TERM>**, or **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.alarm.RuleDetail.AlertLevel">Alert Level</UI-TERM>**.
