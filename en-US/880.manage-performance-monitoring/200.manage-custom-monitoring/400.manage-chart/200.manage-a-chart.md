# Manage charts

On the **Charts** tab, you can edit, copy, and delete a custom chart.

## Prerequisites

You can create a chart by using the following three methods:

* **Method 1**: On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Host.Detail.Monitor.CustomMonitoring">Custom Monitoring</UI-TERM>** page, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Monitor.AddMonitor.VisualizationsTable.CreateAChart">Create Chart</UI-TERM>** in the upper-right corner.
* **Method 2**: If no charts are created, go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Monitor.AddMonitor.CustomMonitoring">Custom Monitoring</UI-TERM>** page, and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Monitor.AddMonitor.MetricTable.CreateAChart">Create Chart</UI-TERM>** on the **Charts** tab.
* **Method 3**: If you have created a metric, go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Monitor.CustomMonitoring">Custom Monitoring</UI-TERM>** page and click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Rule.Detail.Indicators">Metrics</UI-TERM>** tab, where you can create a chart for the metric.

## Procedure

### Edit a chart

OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) allows you to modify the parameters of charts.

1. Log on to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Monitor.Title.PerformanceMonitoring">Performance Monitoring</UI-TERM>**.

3. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Host.Detail.Monitor.CustomMonitoring">Custom Monitoring</UI-TERM>** in the upper-right corner.

4. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Monitor.CustomMonitoring">Custom Monitoring</UI-TERM>** page, click the **Charts** tab.

5. On the **Charts** tab, find the chart that you want to edit and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.Editing">Edit</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.BinlogInstanceList.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column.

6. In the panel that appears on the right, modify the parameters.

7. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.FailoverStandbyTenantModal.Ok">OK</UI-TERM>**.

### Copy a chart

You can quickly create a chart by copying an existing chart and modifying the parameter settings.

1. Log on to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Instance.Monitor.PerformanceMonitoring">Performance Monitoring</UI-TERM>**.

3. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Monitor.CustomMonitoring">Custom Monitoring</UI-TERM>** in the upper-right corner.

4. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Monitor.AddMonitor.CustomMonitoring">Custom Monitoring</UI-TERM>** page, click the **Charts** tab.

5. On the **Charts** tab, find the chart that you want to copy and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.User.Detail.Copy">Copy</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.OBServerList.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column.

6. Optional. In the panel that appears on the right, modify the parameters.

7. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.FailoverStandbyTenantModal.Ok">OK</UI-TERM>**.

### Delete a chart

You can delete a chart that you no longer use.

1. Log on to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Instance.Monitor.PerformanceMonitoring">Performance Monitoring</UI-TERM>**.

3. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Monitor.CustomMonitoring">Custom Monitoring</UI-TERM>** in the upper-right corner.

4. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Monitor.CustomMonitoring">Custom Monitoring</UI-TERM>** page, click the **Charts** tab.

5. On the **Charts** tab, find the chart that you want to delete and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.User.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.TenantList.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column.

6. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.TakeOver.Component.LoadBalancer.Ok">OK</UI-TERM>**.
