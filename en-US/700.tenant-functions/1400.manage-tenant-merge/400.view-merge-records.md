# View the major compaction records

This topic describes how to view the major compaction records of a tenant in the last three days.

## Prerequisites

Make sure that you have the following permissions:

* Resource permissions: **Cluster read-only** or **tenant read-only** permission.
* Menu permissions: Permission on the **Compaction Management** menu of tenants.

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **Tenants**. The **Tenants** tab automatically appears.

3. On the **Tenants** tab, find the target tenant and click its name to go to the **Overview** page of the tenant.

4. In the left-side navigation pane of the page that appears, click **Compaction Management**.

5. In the upper-right corner of the **Details of Major Compaction** or **Configuration for Major Compaction** tab, click **View Major Compaction Records** to view major compaction records of the tenant in the last three days, such as the start time, end time, time consumed, version number, and status of major compactions.
