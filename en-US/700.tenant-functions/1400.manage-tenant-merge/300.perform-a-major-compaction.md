# Perform a major compaction

You can immediately initiate a major compaction for an idle tenant. When an error occurs during the major compaction of the tenant, you can perform troubleshooting, clear the error, and then continue the major compaction.

## Prerequisites

* Make sure that you have the following permissions:
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Maintenance** or **Tenant Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.MergeManagement">Compaction Management</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Tenant.New.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>**

* You can initiate a major compaction only for a primary tenant. The major compaction for a standby tenant is automatically triggered after the standby tenant synchronizes the major compaction status of the primary tenant. You cannot manually initiate a major compaction for a standby tenant.

## Initiate a major compaction

1. Log in to the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Tenant.New.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>**. The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Tenant.New.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>** page automatically appears.

3. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.auditEvent.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>** page, find the target tenant and click its name to go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of the tenant.

4. In the left-side navigation pane of the page that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Compaction.MergeManagement">Compaction Management</UI-TERM>**.

5. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Report.Inspection.ReportContent.BasicInformation">Basic Information</UI-TERM>** section of the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Compaction.MergeDetails">Details of Major Compaction</UI-TERM>** tab, make sure that **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Plan.Component.PlanExecuteTable.Status">Status</UI-TERM>** is **Tenant idle**, and then click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Compaction.Detail.InitiateAMerge">Initiate Major Compaction</UI-TERM>**.

   ![1](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/422/%E7%A7%9F%E6%88%B7%E5%90%88%E5%B9%B61.png)

6. After a major compaction is initiated, the major compaction progress is displayed next to **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Statistics.components.PartitionDrawer.Status">Status</UI-TERM>**.

## Continue the major compaction

When an error occurs during the major compaction of the tenant, perform troubleshooting and then click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Compaction.Detail.ClearException">Clear Exceptions</UI-TERM>** to clear the error flag and continue the major compaction.

<main id="notice" type='notice'>
<h4>Notice</h4>
<p>When an error occurs during the major compaction, we recommend that you perform troubleshooting to locate the cause. If you clear the error flag without troubleshooting the error, the error scope may expand. </p>
</main>

## Suspend the major compaction

If the major compaction of the tenant takes a long time or consumes a large amount of resources in peak hours, you can suspend it. Then, the major compaction status of the tenant changes to **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.task.Paused">Suspended</UI-TERM>**.

## Resume the major compaction

You can click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Compaction.Detail.RestoreMerge">Resume Major Compaction</UI-TERM>** to resume the major compaction in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.task.Paused">Suspended</UI-TERM>** state. Then, the system continues to run the major compaction.
