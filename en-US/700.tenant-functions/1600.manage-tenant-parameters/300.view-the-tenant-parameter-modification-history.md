View the parameter modification history
============================================================

On the Change History tab, you can view the modification records of the parameters of a tenant. The information includes the parameter name, value before and after change of each parameter, the time when a parameter is modified, and the operation user.

## Prerequisites

Make sure that you have the following permissions:

* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Read-only** or **Tenant Read-only** permission
* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.OperationConfig.ParameterManagement">Parameter Management</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Resource.Component.Block.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>**

Procedure
------------------------------

1. Log on to OCP. By default, the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Overview.ClusterOverview">Cluster Overview</UI-TERM>** page appears.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Resource.Unit.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>** .

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Backup.Data.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>** section of the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.ParameterTemplate.DetailDrawer.Tenant">Tenant</UI-TERM>** **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.hook.useMenu.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page, click name of the tenant that you want to view.

4. In the left-side navigation pane of the page that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.ParameterManagement">Parameter Management</UI-TERM>** .

5. Click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.ModifyHistory">Change History</UI-TERM>** tab.

6. You can filter parameters by specifying **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.History.ModificationDate">Date of Modification</UI-TERM>** or **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.ParameterName.1">Parameter Name</UI-TERM>** .

   The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.ParameterTemplate.DetailDrawer.Parameter">Parameter Name</UI-TERM>** field supports fuzzy match.

   ![10291131](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/401/%E4%BF%AE%E6%94%B9%E5%8E%86%E5%8F%B22.png)

7. In the search results, view the history of parameter changes.
