# Delete a tenant replica from a zone

This topic describes how to delete a tenant replica from a zone. After a replica is deleted, its data cannot be restored. Proceed with caution.

## Prerequisites

* Make sure that you have the following permissions:
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Maintenance** or **Tenant Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Resource.Unit.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>**
* In a **standalone centralized** database, you cannot delete a tenant replica from a zone.

## Procedure

1. Log on to the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Resource.Component.Block.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>**. The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.AddDatabaseDrawer.TenantOverview">Tenant Overview</UI-TERM>** page appears.

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Tenant.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>** list, find the target tenant and click its name. The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Iframe.TenantDetails">Tenant Details</UI-TERM>** page appears.

4. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Tenant.Detail.CopyDetails">Replica Details</UI-TERM>** section, as shown in the following figure, you can click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Template.New.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>** indicated by ① to delete a single replica, or select multiple tenant replicas that you want to delete and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Tenant.Detail.BatchDelete">Batch Delete</UI-TERM>** indicated by ② to delete the replicas at a time.

   ![1122](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/410/%E5%88%A0%E9%99%A4%E5%89%AF%E6%9C%AC%E4%B8%AD%E7%9A%84zone-1.png)

5. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.BinlogServerList.DeleteBinlogServerModal.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>**.
