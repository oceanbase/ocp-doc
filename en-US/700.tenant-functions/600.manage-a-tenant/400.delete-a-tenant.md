# Delete a tenant

This topic describes how to delete a tenant. When you delete a tenant from an OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) cluster, the resource pools and data of the tenant are also deleted. A deleted tenant cannot enter the recycle bin, and the data of a deleted tenant cannot be recovered. Proceed with caution.

## Background information

* Make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Maintenance** or **Tenant Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Tenant.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>**

* You can delete only tenants of the primary cluster.

## Procedure

1. Log on to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.DeleteTenantModal.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>** to go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>** page.

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Tenant.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>** list, find the target tenant and click its name to go to its details page.

4. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Tenant.Detail.DeleteATenant">Delete Tenant</UI-TERM>** in the upper-right corner of the page.

   ![10141011](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/422/%E5%88%A0%E9%99%A4%E7%A7%9F%E6%88%B71.png)

5. In the dialog box that appears, enter **delete**.

6. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.DeleteReplicaModal.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>**.
