# Manage OBProxy cluster parameters

On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Iframe.ParameterManagement">Parameter Management</UI-TERM>** page, you can view the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.OperationConfig.Parameter.ParameterList">Parameters</UI-TERM>** and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.OperationConfig.Parameter.ModifyHistory">Change History</UI-TERM>** of an OBProxy cluster.

## Prerequisites

* To view OBProxy cluster parameters and their modification history, make sure that the user who logs in to OCP has the following permissions:

  * **OBProxy Read-Only** resource permission.
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.OperationConfig.ParameterManagement">Parameter Management</UI-TERM>** menu permission.

* To modify OBProxy cluster parameters, make sure that the user who logs in to OCP has the following permissions:

  * **OBProxy Maintenance** resource permission.
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Iframe.ParameterManagement">Parameter Management</UI-TERM>** menu permission.

## View and modify parameters

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **OBProxy**.

3. In the cluster list, find the OBProxy cluster that you want to manage and click its name to go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of the cluster.

4. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.OperationConfig.ParameterManagement">Parameter Management</UI-TERM>**. The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.MultiCluster.Parameter.ParameterList">Parameter List</UI-TERM>** tab appears.

   On this tab, you can:
   * View parameters: Enter the parameter name in the search box and click Search to view the specified parameter. You can also perform fuzzy search.

   * Modify parameters:

     1. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.BinlogService.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column of the target parameter, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.OperationConfig.List.ModifyValue">Change Value</UI-TERM>**.

     2. Enter a value in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.SQLCondition.Value">Value</UI-TERM>** field and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.Event.Determine">OK</UI-TERM>**.

     3. After all values of the target parameter are modified, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.SubmitModification">Submit Changes</UI-TERM>**.

     4. In the panel that appears, confirm the modification information and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.ModifyParameterDrawer.ConfirmModification">Confirm Modification</UI-TERM>**. After the modification task is completed, the modification is successful.

     5. After the parameters are modified, you need to view the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.List.WhetherTheRestartTakesEffect">Restart to Apply Changes</UI-TERM>** information to determine whether the parameters take effect:

        * Parameters that do not require a restart: These parameters take effect about 3 minutes after they are modified.
        * Parameters that require a restart: These parameters take effect after OBProxy is restarted.

      <main id="notice" type='explain'>
      <h4>Note</h4>
      <p><b>Read-only</b> parameters cannot be modified. </p>
      </main>

## View the modification history of a parameter

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **OBProxy**.

3. In the cluster list, find the OBProxy cluster that you want to manage and click its name to go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Monitor.Monitor.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of the cluster.

4. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.ParameterManagement">Parameter Management</UI-TERM>**. The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.MultiAzone.ParameterList">Parameter List</UI-TERM>** tab automatically appears.

5. Click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.ModifyHistory">Change History</UI-TERM>** tab.

   Enter **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.OperationConfig.History.ModifiedDate">Date of Modification</UI-TERM>** and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.History.Parameter.1">Parameter Name</UI-TERM>** to filter parameters and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.SQLDiagnosis.Query">Search</UI-TERM>**. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.ModifyParameterDrawer.Parameter">Parameter Name</UI-TERM>** tab, you can view the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.History.ValueBeforeChange">Value before Change</UI-TERM>** and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.History.ValueAfterChange">Value after Change</UI-TERM>** of the parameter, as well as the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Overview.ModificationTime">Modified At</UI-TERM>** and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.History.OperationUser">Changed By</UI-TERM>**.
