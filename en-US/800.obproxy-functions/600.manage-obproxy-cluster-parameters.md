# Parameter management

You can view and modify parameters on the Parameter Management page.

## Prerequisites

* To view the parameter settings and parameter modification history of an OBProxy cluster, make sure that you have the following permissions:
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.Detail.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **OBProxy Read-only** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.ParameterManagement">Parameter Management</UI-TERM>** menu of **OBProxy**
* To modify the parameters of an OBProxy cluster, make sure that you have the following permissions:
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.Detail.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **OBProxy Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.ParameterManagement">Parameter Management</UI-TERM>** menu of **OBProxy**

## View parameters

You can search for parameters on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.MultiCluster.Parameter.ParameterList">Parameters</UI-TERM>** tab to view the information about specific parameters. You can also view the parameter name, value, description, and whether a restart is required for updates to take effect in the parameter list. You can modify these parameters.

![Image 140](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/403-cn/obproxy%E5%8F%82%E6%95%B0%E7%AE%A1%E7%90%861.png)

1. You can click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.List.ModifyTheValue">Change Value</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column.
2. enter the new value in the dialog box that appears, and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Binlog.Component.FlowBinlogModal.Ok">OK</UI-TERM>** to complete the modification.
3. After the values of the target parameters are modified, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.SubmitModification">Submit Changes</UI-TERM>**. Then, waitting for the modification task execution to complete.

    ![1](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/403-cn/%E4%BF%AE%E6%94%B9%E5%8F%82%E6%95%B01.png)

4. Confirm the modification information on the page, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.ModifyParameterDrawer.ConfirmModification">Confirm Modification</UI-TERM>**. Then, waitting for the modification task execution to complete.

5. After the parameter modification is completed, you need to check the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.List.WhetherTheRestartTakesEffect">Restart to Apply Changes</UI-TERM>** information of the parameter to determine how the parameter takes effect.
  
    * Parameters take effect without restarting: The modification takes about 3 minutes to take effect.
    * Parameters require a restart to take effect: It will take effect after restarting the OBProxy.

      <main id="notice" type='notice'>
      <h4>Notice</h4>
      <p><b>Read-only</b> parameters cannot modify parameter values.</p>
      </main>

## View change history

On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.OperationConfig.Parameter.ModifyHistory">Change History</UI-TERM>** tab, you can specify the following fields: **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.History.ModificationDate">Date of Modification</UI-TERM>** , and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.List.Parameter">Parameter Name</UI-TERM>** . Then, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.User.loginHistory.Query">Search</UI-TERM>** to filter the parameters and find the information you want. In the parameter list, you can view information in the following columns: **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.ParameterTemplate.SetParameterEditableProTable.Parameter">Parameter Name</UI-TERM>** , **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.ModifyParameterDrawer.ValueBeforeChange">Value before Change</UI-TERM>** , **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.History.ValueAfterTheChange">Value after Change</UI-TERM>** , **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Overview.ModificationTime">Modified At</UI-TERM>** , and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.History.User">Changed By</UI-TERM>** .
