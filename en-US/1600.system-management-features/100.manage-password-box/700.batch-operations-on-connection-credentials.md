# Batch operations on credentials

This topic describes how to verify, export, and delete credentials in batches.

## Prerequisites

* To perform batch verification and batch export, make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.Detail.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Read-only**, **Tenant Read-only**, **OBProxy Read-only**, **Binlog Service Read-only**, or **Host Read-only** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.component.AddOBProxyCredentialModal.CredentialManagement">Credential Management</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.SystemManagement">System Management</UI-TERM>**

* To perform batch deletion, make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.Detail.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Maintenance**, **Tenant Maintenance**, **OBProxy Maintenance**, **Binlog Service Maintenance**, or **Host Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.component.AddOBCredentialModal.CredentialManagement">Credential Management</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.SystemManagement">System Management</UI-TERM>**

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.SystemManagement">System Management</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.component.AddOBProxyCredentialModal.CredentialManagement">Credential Management</UI-TERM>** to go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Credential.Cluster.CredentialList">Credentials</UI-TERM>** page.

3. On the **OceanBase**, **OBProxy**, or **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.component.ArchitectureAndVpcAlert.Host">Host</UI-TERM>** tab, select the credentials for batch operation.

4. Click a batch operation type in the upper-right corner of the tab as needed.

   You can click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Credential.Component.FilterBar.BatchVerification">Batch Verify</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Credential.Component.FilterBar.BatchExport">Batch Export</UI-TERM>**, or **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.obproxy.BatchDelete">Batch Delete</UI-TERM>**.

   * To verify the selected credentials at a time, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Credential.Component.FilterBar.BatchVerification">Batch Verify</UI-TERM>** to test whether the credentials can be used for connections.

   * To export the selected credentials at a time, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Credential.Component.FilterBar.BatchExport">Batch Export</UI-TERM>**. In the dialog box that appears, enter the encryption password and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.credential.Export">Export</UI-TERM>**.

   * To delete the selected credentials at a time, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Host.BatchDelete">Batch Delete</UI-TERM>**. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.User.Oracle.RolesList.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>**.
