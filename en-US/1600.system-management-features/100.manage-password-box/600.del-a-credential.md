# Delete a credential

This topic describes how to delete a credential.

<main id="notice" type='explain'>
<h4>Note</h4>
<p>After you delete a credential, the O&amp;M of the corresponding OceanBase cluster, tenant, OBProxy cluster, or host may fail due to the absence of the credential. </p>
</main>

## Prerequisites

Make sure that you have the following permissions:

* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Maintenance**, **Tenant Maintenance**, **OBProxy Maintenance**, **Binlog Service Maintenance**, or **Host Maintenance** permission
* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.component.AddBinlogCredentialModal.CredentialManagement">Credential Management</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.SystemManagement">System Management</UI-TERM>**

## Procedure

1. Log on to the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.SystemManagement">System Management</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.component.AddOBProxyCredentialModal.CredentialManagement">Credential Management</UI-TERM>** to go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Credential.OBProxy.CredentialList">Credentials</UI-TERM>** page.

3. Find the credential to be deleted, hover the pointer over **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Welcome.SeeMore">More</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.LinkList.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column of the credential and choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.component.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>**.

4. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.OBServerList.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>**.
