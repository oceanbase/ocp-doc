# Log off the OCP console

This topic describes how to log off the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console.

## Procedure

1. Click the username in the upper-right corner of the OCP console, and choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Layout.Header.ExitLogin">Log Off</UI-TERM>** .

2. In the confirmation dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.Compaction.MergePolicyDrawer.Ok">OK</UI-TERM>** .
