# Switchover of OCP clusters

This topic describes how to switch an OCP standby cluster to a primary cluster. After the switchover, the primary and standby clusters exchange their roles, but their primary-standby relationship remains.

<main id="notice" type='notice'>
<h4>Applicability</h4>
<p>This topic applies only to OCP Enterprise Edition. OCP Community Edition does not support this feature. </p>
</main>

## Prerequisites

* You have logged in as the **ADMIN** user.
* If you want to delete an OCP primary cluster, you must perform a switchover to switch the primary cluster to a standby cluster, and then delete the standby cluster. For more information, see [Delete an OCP cluster](../300.ocp-multi-cluster-mode/800.remove-an-ocp-cluster.md). Otherwise, perform a switchover only when necessary.

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **System Management\>OCP Multi-Cluster Mode**.

3. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.MultiCluster.MasterSlaveDetails">Leader & Follower Details</UI-TERM>** tab, find the **Current OCP Basic Info** section and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.MultiCluster.Detail.SwitchToPrimaryOcp">Switch to Leader OCP</UI-TERM>** in the Actions column.

   ![1](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/zh-CN/0800872261/p280235.png)

4. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.MultiCluster.Detail.SwitchoverModal.DailySwitchover">Switchover</UI-TERM>**.

   ![2](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/zh-CN/0800872261/p280219.png)

5. After the switchover is completed, you can view the progress in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Layout.BasicLayout.TaskCenter">Task Center</UI-TERM>**. For more information, see [Manage tasks](../../1600.system-management-features/600.manage-tasks.md).
