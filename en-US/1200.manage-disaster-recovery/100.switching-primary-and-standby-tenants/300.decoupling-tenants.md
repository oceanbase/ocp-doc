# Decouple primary and standby tenants

This topic describes how to decouple a standby tenant from its primary tenant and convert the standby tenant into an independent primary tenant.

## Prerequisites

* You have logged in to the OceanBase Cloud console as a user with the following permissions:

  * **Cluster Maintenance** or **Tenant Maintenance** permission.
  * Permission to access the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.SummaryInvestingComZa">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of a tenant.

* The current tenant is a standby tenant.

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OceanBase Cloud console and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Event.Detail.Tenant">Tenant</UI-TERM>** in the left-side navigation pane. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Tenant.TenantList">Tenants</UI-TERM>** page, find the standby tenant and click its name.

2. In the upper-right corner of the page that appears, click **More** and select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Overview.ActiveStandbyDecoupling">Decouple Primary and Standby Clusters</UI-TERM>**.

   ![1](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/431/%E4%B8%BB%E5%A4%87%E8%A7%A3%E8%80%A6.png)

3. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.SQLDiagnosis.Component.FieldListDrawer.Determine">OK</UI-TERM>** to initiate the decoupling task.

    <main id="notice" type='notice'>
    <h4>Notice</h4>
    <p>To avoid unexpected issues, the system sets the service name of the new primary tenant and its downstream tenants to empty by default. After decoupling, the standby tenant will exist as an independent tenant and will no longer synchronize data from the original primary tenant. Proceed with caution.</p>
    </ul>
    </main>

4. After the decoupling is successful, the standby tenant will become a new primary tenant. You can view the details of this decoupling event in [View Operation Audit](../../1600.system-management-features/400.view-operation-audit.md).

## References

In OCP multi-cluster mode, after the primary and standby tenants are decoupled, the kms_host of the new primary tenant will change. You need to refer to [Manage TDE Transparent Encryption](../../600.cluster-functions/1200.manage-security-configuration/200.manage-tde-transparent-encryption.md) to update the TDE encryption configuration of the new primary tenant in a timely manner to avoid the failure of standby tenant creation due to the absence of TDE keys.
