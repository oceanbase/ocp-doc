# Stop an OBServer service

This topic describes how to stop an OBServer service.

## Prerequisites

* Make sure that you have the following permissions:
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.OBProxy.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>**

* Your OceanBase cluster is managed in the current OCP cluster.

   Otherwise, you need to use the current OCP cluster to take over your OceanBase cluster. For more information, see [Take over a cluster](../300.manage-a-cluster/400.take-over-a-cluster.md).

* (Optional) If you don't want to affect the business when the OBServer is stopped, you need to have other OBServers in the zone where the OBServer to be stopped is located, and ensure sufficient resources. At this point, stop the OBServer, and the Unit will automatically switch to other OBServers, thus not affecting the business.

## Procedure

1. Log on to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>**. The cluster overview page appears.

3. On the Clusters page, find the target cluster that includes the OBServer you want to stop in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** list and click its name. The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of the cluster appears.

4. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Component.MachineResource.NumberOfObservers">OBServers</UI-TERM>** list,  choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.oceanbase.StopService">Stop Service</UI-TERM>**.

   ![Image 169](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/%E5%81%9C%E6%AD%A2%E6%9C%8D%E5%8A%A1.png)

5. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ZoneList.Stop">Stop</UI-TERM>** to stop the OBServer service.

   In the dialog box, you can click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Import.ImportClusterResults.ViewTasks">View Task</UI-TERM>** to view the progress.

   You can also choose **System Management > Tasks** to view the progress of the task.

   When the task status is **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.plan.Completed">Completed</UI-TERM>** and the status of the OBServer is **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.binlog.Stopped.3">Stopped</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Component.MachineResource.NumberOfObservers">OBServers</UI-TERM>** list on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page, the OBServer is stopped.
