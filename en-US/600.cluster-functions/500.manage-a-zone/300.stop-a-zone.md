# Stop a zone

This topic describes how to stop a zone. Stopping a zone stops all OBServer nodes in the zone. Proceed with caution.

## Prerequisites

* The login user has the following permissions:

  * The **Cluster Maintenance** resource permission.
  * The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.hook.useMenu.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** menu permission of the cluster.

* Your OceanBase cluster is managed in the current OCP cluster.

  If not, you can use the current OCP cluster to take over your OceanBase cluster. For more information, see [Take over a cluster](../300.manage-a-cluster/400.take-over-a-cluster.md).

* Standalone mode does not support the stop operation.

* Make sure that no primary tenant replicas are hosted on the zone to be stopped. If primary tenant replicas are hosted on the zone, make sure that the tenant has other full-featured replicas available. Otherwise, the business will be interrupted if the zone is stopped.

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Monitor.Cluster">Cluster</UI-TERM>**. The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Component.ClusterList.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** page automatically appears.

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.OBProxy.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** list on the page, find the target cluster and click its name.

4. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.SummaryInvestingComZa">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of the cluster, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Oracle.UserOrRoleDetail.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** > **More > Stop** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ZoneList.ZoneList">Zones</UI-TERM>** section.

   ![11181758](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/421/%E5%81%9C%E6%AD%A2zone.png)

5. In the dialog box that appears, specify whether to perform a minor compaction before stopping the OBServer nodes (the default option is to perform a minor compaction), and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Restore.SamplingStrategy.Detail.Stop">Stop</UI-TERM>**.

   A minor compaction prolongs the response time required to stop the OBServer nodes but significantly shortens the time required to restore the OBServer nodes.

6. After the task is submitted, you can click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.ViewTasks">View Tasks</UI-TERM>** in the message that appears to view the task progress, or view the task progress on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Layout.BasicLayout.TaskCenter">Task Center</UI-TERM>** page.

   When the task status is **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Log.TaskGraph.Complete">Complete</UI-TERM>**, and the status of the zone is **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.oceanbase.Stopped">Stopped</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ZoneList.ZoneList">Zones</UI-TERM>** section of the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Overview.Statistics.SummaryInvestingComZa">Overview</UI-TERM>** page, the zone is stopped.

7. (Optional) If you need to use the zone, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.obproxy.Start">Start</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ZoneList.ZoneList">Zones</UI-TERM>** section to enable the zone.
