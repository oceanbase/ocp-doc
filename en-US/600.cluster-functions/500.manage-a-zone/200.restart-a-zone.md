# Restart a zone

This topic describes how to restart a zone. Restarting a zone restarts all OBServer nodes in the zone. Proceed with caution.

## Prerequisites

* You have logged in to OCP as a user with the following permissions:

  * The **Cluster Maintenance** resource permission.
  * The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** menu permission of the cluster.

* Your OceanBase cluster is managed in the current OCP cluster.

  Otherwise, you need to use the current OCP cluster to take over your OceanBase cluster. For more information, see [Take over a cluster](../300.manage-a-cluster/400.take-over-a-cluster.md).

* You cannot restart a standalone cluster.

## Procedure

<main id="notice" type='notice'>
<h4>Notice</h4>
<p>If the zone to be restarted hosts the primary tenant replica, the tenant's business may be temporarily interrupted during the restart. </p>
</main>

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.ParameterTemplate.Cluster">Cluster</UI-TERM>**. The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.OBProxy.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** page automatically appears.

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Component.ClusterList.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** list on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.OBProxy.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** page, find the target cluster and click its name.

4. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of the cluster, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.TakeOver.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** > **More** > **Restart** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ZoneList.ZoneList">Zones</UI-TERM>** section.

   ![11181800](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/421/%E9%87%8D%E5%90%AFzone.png)

5. In the dialog box that appears, specify whether to perform a minor compaction before the OBServer nodes are stopped (the default is to perform a minor compaction), and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ZoneList.Restart">Restart</UI-TERM>**.

   A minor compaction prolongs the response time for stopping the OBServer nodes, but significantly shortens the time required to restore the OBServer nodes.

6. After the task is submitted, you can click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Welcome.ViewTasks">View Tasks</UI-TERM>** in the dialog box to view the task progress, or view the task progress in **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Layout.BasicLayout.TaskCenter">Task Center</UI-TERM>**.

   When the task status is **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Log.TaskGraph.Complete">Complete</UI-TERM>**, and the status of the zone is **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Statistics.utils.Running">Running</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ZoneList.ZoneList">Zones</UI-TERM>** section of the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of the cluster, the restart is successful.
