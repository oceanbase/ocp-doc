# Replace the arbitration service for a cluster

This topic describes how to replace a failed arbitration service with a new one when the server hosting the arbitration service fails.

<main id="notice" type='notice'>
<h4>Applicability</h4>
<p>This topic applies only to OCP Enterprise Edition. OCP Community Edition does not support the arbitration service. </p>
</main>

## Prerequisites

* The login user in the OCP console has the following permissions:

  * **Cluster Maintenance** permissions on resources.
  * Permissions on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** menu.

* The new arbitration service is in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.obproxy.NormalOperation">Running</UI-TERM>** state.
* Log streams of the tenant associated with the arbitration service are not downgraded.
* The primary and secondary version numbers of the OceanBase cluster and the new arbitration service are the same. For example, the version of the OceanBase cluster and the new arbitration service are both V4.2.x.x.

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>**. The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** page automatically appears.

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Component.ClusterList.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** list on the page, find the target cluster and click its name.

4. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.hook.useMenu.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of the cluster, find the arbitration service that needs to be replaced in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ArbitrationServerList.ArbitrationServiceList">Arbitration Service List</UI-TERM>** section and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.oceanbase.ReplacementService">Replace Service</UI-TERM>** in the Actions column.

5. In the panel that appears, select an arbitration service. You can also click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.New.CreateAService">Create Service</UI-TERM>** at the bottom of the drop-down list to create an arbitration service for the cluster. For more information, see [Create an arbitration service](../../1350.manage-arbitration-services/200.create-arbitration-services.md).

    <main id="notice" type='notice'>
    <h4>Notice</h4>
    <p>During the replacement, the arbitration service is unavailable, which may affect the high availability feature of the tenant replicas. </p>
    </main>

6. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.List.Submitted">Submit</UI-TERM>**.
