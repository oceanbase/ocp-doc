# Change the password
You can change the password of the root user in the sys tenant of a cluster you manage based on your business requirements.

## Prerequisites

Ensure that the currently logged-in account has cluster management permissions.
## Procedure

1. Log in to OCP.

2. In the left navigation bar, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.Cluster">Cluster</UI-TERM>** to go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Plan.Component.PlanTemplateTable.Cluster">Cluster</UI-TERM>** page.
3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.OBProxy.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** section, select the target cluster and click its name.

4. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page, click the **...** icon in the upper-right corner and select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Layout.Header.ChangePassword">Change Password</UI-TERM>**.

5. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.component.ModifyUserPasswordModal.ChangePassword">Change Password</UI-TERM>** dialog box, enter the new password and confirm it.

The password must meet the following requirements: 8 to 32 characters in length, support letters, numbers, and special characters, and contain at least two uppercase letters, two lowercase letters, two digits, and two special characters. Supported special characters are \~!@#%\^\&\*_-+=\`\|(){}\[\]:;',.?/.

6. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.User.Oracle.UserList.Determine">OK</UI-TERM>**.
