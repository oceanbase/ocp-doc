# Take over a cluster

You can take over a cluster created through a CLI or another OCP cluster to the current OCP cluster. After the cluster is taken over, you can manage and maintain it in the current OCP cluster.

## Prerequisites

* The user who logs in to OCP must be assigned the **ADMIN** or **ORG_ADMIN** role.

* All OBServer nodes in the OceanBase cluster to be taken over must be running.

* When you take over an OceanBase cluster, you must follow the principle of taking over the primary cluster first:

  * In a single OCP scenario, you must take over the primary OceanBase cluster first, and then take over the standby OceanBase cluster.
  * In a multi-OCP scenario, you must take over the primary OceanBase cluster in one OCP first, and then take over the standby OceanBase cluster in any OCP.

* The tenant service names in the OceanBase cluster to be taken over must be unique in the system. Otherwise, the service names of the tenants with name conflicts in the cluster will be marked as **Invalid**. For more information about service names, see [Manage service names](../../700.tenant-functions/600.manage-a-tenant/800.manage-service-name.md).

* If the OceanBase cluster to be taken over is deployed by using obd, you must check whether the cluster meets the takeover conditions. For more information, see [Take over an obd-deployed cluster](https://www.oceanbase.com/docs/community-obd-cn-1000000001477802).

* If the OceanBase cluster to be taken over has an arbitration service configured, you must migrate the arbitration service out of the cluster and take it over to the new OCP before you take over the cluster. For more information, see [Migrate an arbitration service](../1350.manage-arbitration-services/700.migrate-an-arbitration-service.md) and [Take over an arbitration service](../1350.manage-arbitration-services/250.take-over-arbitration-serveices.md).

## Considerations

* Only one OCP can manage an OceanBase cluster. After you take over an OceanBase cluster, it cannot be managed or monitored in the original OCP. Therefore, you must evaluate whether to take over the cluster before you perform the operation. We recommend that you migrate the cluster out before you take it over. For more information, see [Migrate a cluster](650.migrate-a-cluster.md).
* When you take over an OceanBase cluster, OCP takes over the hosts corresponding to the OBServer nodes in the cluster for unified resource management.
  * If the credentials used by the hosts are consistent, you can take over the hosts in the **Precheck** step.
  * If the credentials used by the hosts are inconsistent, you must take over the hosts in [Add a host](../../850.host-features/100.manage-a-host/200.add-a-host.md) first, and then take over the cluster.
* If the OceanBase cluster and OBProxy cluster share the same host, you must perform the following operations in the order specified when you migrate or take over the cluster. For more information, see [OCP IDC migration](../../1850.ocp-om-best-practices/750.ocp-idc-migration.md).
   1. [Migrate an OBProxy cluster](../../800.obproxy-functions/300.manage-a-obproxy-cluster/450.migrate-an-obproxy-cluster.md).
   2. [Migrate an OceanBase cluster](650.migrate-a-cluster.md).
   3. Take over the OceanBase cluster.
   4. [Take over an OBProxy server](../../800.obproxy-functions/400.manage-a-obproxy-server/200.take-over-an-obproxy.md).

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.alarm.Cluster">Cluster</UI-TERM>**. The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.OBProxy.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** page automatically appears.

3. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** page, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.TakeOverTheCluster">Take over Cluster</UI-TERM>** in the upper-right corner.

4. Connect to the cluster. You can connect to the cluster by using **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Import.DirectlyConnected">Direct Connection</UI-TERM>** or **OBProxy**.

   * **Direct Connection:** Enter the cluster's **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.OBProxy.Endpoint">Access URL</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Scan.ReportTable.ServerInfoTable.Port">Port</UI-TERM>**, and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Import.RootSysPassword">Root@sys Password</UI-TERM>**. You can also choose to **Enter the proxyro account password**. Then, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.StepsMoveOutCluster.Step1.NextStep">Next</UI-TERM>**.

For more information about the proxyro account, see <a href="../../800.obproxy-functions/300.manage-a-obproxy-cluster/900.proxyro-user-management.md">Manage the proxyro account</a>. </p>
      </main>

   * **OBProxy:** Enter the cluster's **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.MultiCluster.Detail.Endpoint">Access URL</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Import.Port">Port</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.SwitchModal.ClusterName">Cluster Name</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Import.ClusterIdOptional">Cluster ID (Optional)</UI-TERM>**, and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Import.RootSysPassword">Root@sys Password</UI-TERM>**. You can also choose to **Enter the proxyro account password**. Then, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.ResourceManager.CreateResourcePlan.NextStep">Next</UI-TERM>**.

5. After the cluster is connected, the takeover precheck is performed.

   * If the precheck is passed, but some OBServer nodes are not managed by OCP, enter the relevant information and take over the nodes.

   * If the precheck fails, modify the OBServer node configurations as prompted and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Import.ReCheck">Recheck</UI-TERM>**.

   ![09010928](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/401/%E6%8E%A5%E7%AE%A1%E9%9B%86%E7%BE%A41.png)

6. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Settings.Component.ModifyUserInfoDrawer.Submit">Submit</UI-TERM>**.

   After the task is submitted, you can view the task execution progress on the task result page or return to the cluster overview page.

## What to do next

* If the cluster to be taken over has a load type configured, you need to reconfigure the load type on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.hook.useMenu.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of the cluster after it is taken over.
* If TDE encryption is enabled for tenants in the cluster, you need to configure TDE encryption for the tenants in the current OCP instance after the cluster is taken over. Otherwise, you cannot add OBServer nodes, create standby tenants, or create encrypted tablespaces.
* After the cluster is taken over, you need to reconfigure the backup strategy based on the original cluster or tenant's backup strategy.
