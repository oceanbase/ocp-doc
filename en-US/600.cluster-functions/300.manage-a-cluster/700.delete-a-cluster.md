# Delete a cluster

You can delete an OceanBase cluster as needed.

## Prerequisite

Make sure that you have the following permissions:

* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Management** permission
* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.OBProxy.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>**

## Procedure

1. Log on to the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.OBProxy.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>**.

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.OBProxy.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** list, find the target cluster and click its name.

4. In the upper-right corner of the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Overview.Statistics.SummaryInvestingComZa">Overview</UI-TERM>** page, click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.AddBackupStrategy.ViewMore">More</UI-TERM>** icon, and select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Topo.TopoGraph.DeleteACluster">Delete Cluster</UI-TERM>** .

5. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.DeleteModal.DeleteACluster">Delete Cluster</UI-TERM>** dialog box, enter the word "delete" and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.ResourceManager.ResourcePlanTab.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>** .
