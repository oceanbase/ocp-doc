# Replace an arbitration service

This topic describes how to replace a failed arbitration service with a new one when the server hosting the arbitration service fails. Before you replace an arbitration service, note the following items:

* The replacement may affect the high availability feature of associated tenant replicas. We recommend that you perform the operation during off-peak hours.
* Ensure that the specifications of the new arbitration service are sufficient for business load. In scenarios such as insufficient disk space, the arbitration service may not be able to support business load.

<main id="notice" type='notice'>
<h4>Applicability</h4>
<p>This topic applies only to OCP Enterprise Edition. OCP Community Edition does not support the arbitration service. </p>
</main>

## Prerequisites

* The login user in the OCP console has the following permissions:

  * The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.obproxy.MaintainArbitrationService">Maintain Arbitration Service</UI-TERM>** resource permission.
  * The permission to access the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Overview.Statistics.SummaryInvestingComZa">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of the cluster.

* The new arbitration service is in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.obproxy.NormalOperation">Running</UI-TERM>** state.
* Log streams of the tenants associated with the arbitration service are not downgraded.
* If the arbitration service is associated with an OceanBase cluster, the new arbitration service must have the same major and minor versions as the OceanBase cluster. For example, if the versions of both the OceanBase cluster and the arbitration service are V4.2.x.x, otherwise the replacement will fail.

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-performance-report.pages.Report.Content.Cluster">Cluster</UI-TERM>** to go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Component.ClusterList.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** page.

3. Click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.plan.ArbitrationService">Arbitration Service</UI-TERM>** tab.

4. Find the arbitration service that you want to replace and choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.AddSamplingInspectStrategy.More">More</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.oceanbase.ReplacementService">Replace Service</UI-TERM>** in the Actions column.

5. In the panel that appears, select an arbitration service. You can also click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.New.CreateAService">Create Service</UI-TERM>** at the bottom of the drop-down list to create an arbitration service for the cluster. For more information, see [Create an arbitration service](200.create-arbitration-services.md).

6. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Parameter.List.Submitted">Submit</UI-TERM>**.
