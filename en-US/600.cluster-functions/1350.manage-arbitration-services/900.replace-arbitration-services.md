# Replace an arbitration service

This topic describes how to replace a failed arbitration service with a new one when the server hosting the arbitration service fails.

<main id="notice" type='notice'>
<h4>Applicability</h4>
<p>This topic applies only to OceanBase Cloud Platform Enterprise Edition. OceanBase Cloud Platform Community Edition does not support the arbitration service. </p>
</main>

## Prerequisites

* Make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Overview.Statistics.SummaryInvestingComZa">Overview</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>**

* The new arbitration service is in the **RUNNING** state.
* Log streams of the tenants associated with the arbitration service are not downgraded.

## Procedure

1. Log on to the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>**.

3. Click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Org.SharedResourceDrawer.ArbitrationService">Arbitration Service</UI-TERM>** tab to view the cluster arbitration service list.

4. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ArbitrationServerList.ArbitrationServiceList">Arbitration Service List</UI-TERM>** section on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Overview.ClusterOverview">Cluster Overview</UI-TERM>** page, find the arbitration service that you want to replace and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.oceanbase.ReplacementService">Replace Service</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Inspection.Component.TaskTable.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column.

5. In the panel that appears, select an arbitration service. You can also click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.New.CreateAService">Create Service</UI-TERM>** at the bottom of the drop-down list to create an arbitration service for the cluster. For more information, see [Create an arbitration service](200.create-arbitration-services.md).

   <main id="notice" type='notice'>
   <h4>Notice</h4>
   <p>During the replacement, the arbitration service is unavailable, which may affect the high availability feature of the tenant replicas. </p>
   </main>

6. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.AddDatabaseModal.Submitted">Submit</UI-TERM>**.
