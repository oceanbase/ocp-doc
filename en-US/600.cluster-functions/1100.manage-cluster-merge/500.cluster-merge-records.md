# View the major compaction records

This topic describes how to view the major compaction records of a cluster in the last three days.

## Prerequisites

Make sure that you have the following permissions:

* Resource permissions: **Cluster read-only** permission.
* Menu permissions: Permission on the **Compaction Management** menu of clusters.

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **Clusters**. The **Clusters** tab automatically appears.

3. On the **Clusters** page, find the target cluster and click its name to go to the **Overview** page.

4. In the left-side navigation pane, click **Compaction Management**.

5. In the upper-right corner of the **Details of Major Compaction** or **Configuration for Major Compaction** tab, click **View Major Compaction Records** to view major compaction records of the cluster in the last three days, such as the tenant name and ID, start time, end time, time consumed, version number, and status of major compactions.
