# Perform a major compaction

You can immediately trigger a major compaction for a cluster in the idle state. When an error occurs during the major compaction of the cluster, you can perform troubleshooting to clear the error, and then continue with the major compaction.

## Prerequisites

* Make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.Detail.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Compaction.MergeManagement">Compaction Management</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>**

* Cluster-level major compactions are no longer supported in OceanBase Database V4.0 and later. You can initiate a major compaction only for a primary tenant in a cluster. The major compaction for a standby tenant is automatically triggered after the standby tenant synchronizes the major compaction status of the primary tenant. You cannot manually initiate a major compaction for a standby tenant.

* The `enable_manual_merge` parameter specifies whether to enable manual major compactions. If the cluster version is between OceanBase Database V1.x and V3.x, make sure that the `enable_manual_merge` parameter is set to `False` on the page that appears after you choose **Clusters > Parameter Management**. Alternatively, if `enable_manual_merge` is set to `True`, manually perform a major compaction by using SQL statements. For more information, see [Manually control a major compaction](https://www.oceanbase.com/docs/enterprise-oceanbase-database-cn-0000000001417800).

## Procedure

1. Log on to the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Component.ClusterList.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** to go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** page.

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** list on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.OBProxy.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** page, find the target cluster and click its name.

4. In the left-side navigation pane of the page that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Compaction.MergeManagement">Compaction Management</UI-TERM>**.

5. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Compaction.MergeDetails">Details of Major Compaction</UI-TERM>** tab, view the major compaction information in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Transaction.Detail.BasicInformation">Basic Information</UI-TERM>** section.

   ![Perform major compaction](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/420/420-en/%E5%90%88%E5%B9%B6%E8%AF%A6%E6%83%85.png)

6. After you confirm that the cluster is in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Compaction.Detail.MiniCompactionChart.Idle">Idle</UI-TERM>** state, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Compaction.Detail.InitiateAMerge">Initiate Major Compaction</UI-TERM>**. The system will initiate a major compaction for all tenants in the cluster.

   After a major compaction is initiated, the major compaction progress is displayed next to **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Plan.Component.PlanTemplateTable.Status">Status</UI-TERM>**.

7. If the status is **Tenant major compaction in progress**, you can click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Compaction.DetailV4.ViewTenants">View Tenants</UI-TERM>** to view the tenants that are in the progress of the major compaction.

8. When an error occurs during the major compaction of the tenants, you can click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Compaction.DetailV4.ViewTenants">View Tenants</UI-TERM>** to locate the faulty tenant.
   After you perform troubleshooting, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Compaction.DetailV4.ClearException">Clear Exceptions</UI-TERM>** to clear the error flag and continue the major compaction.

   <main id="notice" type='notice'>
   <h4>Notice</h4>
   <p>When an error occurs during a major compaction, we recommend that you contact a professional to verify the problem. If you clear the error flag without troubleshooting the error, the error scope may expand. </p>
   </main>
