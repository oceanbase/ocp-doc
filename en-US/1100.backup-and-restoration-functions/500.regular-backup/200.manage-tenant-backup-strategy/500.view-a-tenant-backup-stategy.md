# View a tenant-level backup stategy

This section introduces you how to view the detailed configuration of the tenant-level backup strategy.

## Prerequisites

Make sure that you have the following permissions:

* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.Detail.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Read-only** or **Tenant Read-only** permission
* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Tenant.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>**

## Procedure

1. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Resource.Component.Block.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>** to go to the Tenant Overview page.

2. Find the target tenant in the Tenants list, and click its name. In the left-side navigation pane of the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.AddDatabaseDrawer.TenantOverview">Tenant Overview</UI-TERM>** page, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.alarm.BackupAndRecovery">Backup and Recovery</UI-TERM>**.

3. Click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Backup.BackupPolicy">Backup Strategy</UI-TERM>** tab to view the details of the backup strategy.
