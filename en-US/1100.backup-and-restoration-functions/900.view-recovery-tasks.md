# View restore task information

This topic describes how to view restore task information.

You can view restore task information by using the following methods:

* **Method 1**: Log in to the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Guide.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.backup.Recovery">Recovery</UI-TERM>**. You can view restore task information on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Overview.Task.RestoreTask">Recovery Tasks</UI-TERM>** tab.

* **Method 2**: Log in to the OCP console and go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Overview.Statistics.SummaryInvestingComZa">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of a cluster. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Component.RestoreNow.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>**. You can view the restore information of the cluster on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Restore.RestoreTask">Recovery Tasks</UI-TERM>** tab.

* **Method 3**: Log in to the OCP console and go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page of a tenant. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Guide.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>**. You can view the restore information of the tenant on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Overview.Task.RestoreTask">Recovery Tasks</UI-TERM>** tab.

## Prerequisites

* To view restore task information by using **Method 1**, make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Read-only** or **Tenant Read-only** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **Restore** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.BackupStrategy.New.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>**

* To view restore task information by using **Method 2**, make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Read-only** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.BackupStrategy.Edit.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>**

* To view restore task information by using **Method 3**, make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Read-only** or **Tenant Read-only** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Component.RestoreNow.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>**

## Procedure

The procedure of **Method 1** is described as follows:

1. Log in to the OCP console.

2. In the left-side navigation page, choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>** \> **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Restore.Recovery">Recovery</UI-TERM>**. The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Restore.Recovery">Recovery</UI-TERM>** tab appears.

3. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Restore.RestoreTask">Recovery Tasks</UI-TERM>** tab, view the task information in the following columns: **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Session.Deadlock.DeadlockHistoryTable.Id">ID</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.AddBackupStrategy.BackupCluster">Backup Cluster</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Restore.RestoreTask.BackupTenant">Backup Tenant</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Restore.RestoreTask.BackupMethod">Backup Method</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Component.RestoreTenantCard.TargetCluster">Destination Cluster</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Restore.SamplingStrategy.TargetTenant">Destination Tenant</UI-TERM>**, **Object to Restore**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Backup.Data.StartTime">Started At</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Information.Detail.CollectDetails.EndTime">Ended At</UI-TERM>**, and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Compaction.DetailV4.CompactionTenantListMyDrawer.Status">Status</UI-TERM>**. You can also perform operations in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Package.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column and filter the restore tasks by status. For more information, see [Initiate a restore task](../1100.backup-and-restoration-functions/700.initiate-a-recovery-task.md) and [Create a sampling strategy for restore](800.regular-recovery/100.create-a-sampling-strategy-for-recovery.md).

    * You can click **Clean Up** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Arbitration.ArbitrationGroup.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column to release the resources occupied by failed restore tasks.

    * You can click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.backup.ViewReasons">View Cause</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Rule.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column to check the causes of the failure.

    * In the upper-right corner of the tab, you can select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.SQLDiagnosis.Digest.LastHour">Last 1 Hour</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.component.RangeTimeDropdown.LastDay">Last 24 Hours</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.component.RangeTimeDropdown.LastWeek">Last 7 Days</UI-TERM>**, or **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.backup.NearlyDays">Last 30 Days</UI-TERM>**, or select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.OCPRangePicker.CustomTime">Custom Time</UI-TERM>** and specify a time range, to view the tasks in the specified time range.
