Backup and restore guide
===========================

This topic describes how to use the backup and restore guide.

## Prerequisites

Make sure that you have the following permissions:

* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Read-only** or **Tenant Read-only** permission
* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Overview.Statistics.SummaryInvestingComZa">Overview</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.package.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>**

Procedure
-------------------------

1. Log on to the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>** **\>** **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>**.

   Check whether the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Overview.Welcome.WelcomeToTheBackupAnd">Welcome to the Backup & Recovery Guide</UI-TERM>** page is displayed.

   ![nav](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/3904306461/p384259.png)

   * If yes, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Overview.Welcome.UseNow">Use Now</UI-TERM>**.

   * If not, click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.AddBackupStrategy.ViewMore">More</UI-TERM>** icon in the upper-right corner and select **Backup Recovery Guide**.

3. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Guide.Wizard">Guide</UI-TERM>** page, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.InstallService.Mounting">Install</UI-TERM>** or **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.AddBackupStrategy.Skip">Skip</UI-TERM>**.

   The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Guide.Wizard">Guide</UI-TERM>** page will guide you through a series of steps. For more information, see [Create a cluster-level backup strategy](500.regular-backup/100.manage-cluster-backup-strategy/100.create-a-cluster-backup-strategy.md), [Create a tenant-level backup strategy](500.regular-backup/200.manage-tenant-backup-strategy/100.creat-a-tenant-backup-stategy.md), and [Create a sampling strategy for recovery](800.regular-recovery/100.create-a-sampling-strategy-for-recovery.md).

   * To perform the corresponding steps, see the corresponding topics.

   * You can also click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.InstallService.Skip">Skip</UI-TERM>** at the lower part of the page if you do not need to perform these steps.
