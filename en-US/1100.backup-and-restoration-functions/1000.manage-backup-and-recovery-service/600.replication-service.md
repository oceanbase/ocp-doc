Copy a service
===================================

To create a service similar to an existing one, you can copy the existing service, and then configure the settings that are different.

Prerequisite
---------------------------------

* Make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Host Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.Service">Service</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.auditEvent.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>**

* You have installed a service. For more information, see [Install a service](../1000.manage-backup-and-recovery-service/200.installation-services.md).

Procedure
------------------------------

1. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Backup \& Recovery** **\>** **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Service.Install.Service">Service</UI-TERM>** .

2. Find the service to copy. Click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Welcome.SeeMore">More</UI-TERM>** icon in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.CreateTagDrawer.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column, and then select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Component.RestoreNow.Copy">Copy</UI-TERM>** to

   go to the **Replicate Service** page.

   On this page, you can edit parameters that are different from those of the copied service. For more information about the parameters, see [Install a service](../1000.manage-backup-and-recovery-service/200.installation-services.md).

3. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.InstallService.Mounting">Install</UI-TERM>** .
