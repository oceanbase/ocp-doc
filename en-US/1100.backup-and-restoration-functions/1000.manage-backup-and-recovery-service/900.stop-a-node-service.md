# Stop a node service
This section describes how to stop the backup and restore service on a host.

## Prerequisites

You have installed the backup and restore service by following [Install the service](../1000.manage-backup-and-recovery-service/200.installation-services.md).
## Procedure

1. In the left navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Guide.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Restore</UI-TERM>** **\>** **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.alarm.Service">Service</UI-TERM>**.

2. Find the target service and click its name.
The system redirects to the service details page.

3. In the **Service Nodes** list, find the node you want to stop and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Restore.SamplingStrategy.Stop">Stop</UI-TERM>**.

The system prompts "Are you sure you want to stop the service for node 'xxx.xxx.xxx.xxx'?"

   <!-- ![ser_stop](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/zh-CN/5337270261/p268008.png) -->

   3. In the Service Node list, find the node you want to stop and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Restore.SamplingStrategy.Stop">Stop</UI-TERM>**.

The system prompts "Are you sure you want to stop the service for node «xxx.xxx.xxx.xxx»?"

   4. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Backup.Log.Stop">Stop</UI-TERM>**.

4. Click **Stop**.
