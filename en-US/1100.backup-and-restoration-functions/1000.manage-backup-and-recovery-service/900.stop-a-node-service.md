Stop a service on a node
=============================================

This topic describes how to stop a backup or recovery service on a host.

Prerequisite
---------------------------------

* Make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.Detail.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Host Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.Detail.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Service.Detail.Service">Service</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.BackupStrategy.Edit.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>**

* You have installed a backup or recovery service. For more information, see [Install a service](../1000.manage-backup-and-recovery-service/200.installation-services.md).

Procedure
------------------------------

1. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Backup \& Recovery** **\>** **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Service.Clone.Service">Service</UI-TERM>** .

2. Find the service to stop and click its name.

   ![11011541](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/1014306461/p346247.png)The service details page appears.

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Service.Detail.ServiceNode">Node Settings</UI-TERM>** list, find the node on which the service to stop is installed and click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ZoneList.Stop">Stop</UI-TERM>** .

   The system prompts: "Are you sure that you want to stop the service on the node xxx.xxx.xxx.xxx?"

4. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ZoneList.Stop">Stop</UI-TERM>** .
