Uninstall a service on a node
==================================================

This topic describes how to uninstall a backup or recovery service on a host.

Prerequisites
----------------------------------

* Make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Host Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Service.Clone.Service">Service</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.auditEvent.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>**

* You have installed a backup or recovery service. For more information, see [Install a service](../1000.manage-backup-and-recovery-service/200.installation-services.md).

* You have stopped the service on the node. For more information, see [Stop a service on a node](900.stop-a-node-service.md).

Procedure
------------------------------

1. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Backup \& Recovery** **\>** **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Subscriptions.TargetLayout.Service">Service</UI-TERM>** .

2. Find the service to uninstall and click its name.

   The service details page appears.

   ![11011547](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/5014306461/p346267.png)

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Service.Detail.ServiceNode">Node Settings</UI-TERM>** list, find the node to uninstall the service from. Click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Welcome.SeeMore">More</UI-TERM>** icon and select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.backup.Uninstall">Uninstall</UI-TERM>** .

   The system prompts: "Are you sure that you want to uninstall the service on the node xxx.xxx.xxx.xxx?"

4. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.backup.Uninstall">Uninstall</UI-TERM>** .
