Delete a service
=====================================

This topic describes how to delete a backup or recovery service that you no longer need.

Prerequisites
----------------------------------

* Make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.Detail.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Host Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.alarm.Service">Service</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.menu.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>**

* You have installed a backup or recovery service. For more information, see [Install a service](../1000.manage-backup-and-recovery-service/200.installation-services.md).

* You have uninstalled the service to delete on all of the nodes where the service is installed. For more information, see [Uninstall a service on a node](1200.uninstalls-a-node.md).

Procedure
------------------------------

1. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Backup \& Recovery** **\>** **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Service.Clone.Service">Service</UI-TERM>** .

2. Find the service to delete. Click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Welcome.SeeMore">More</UI-TERM>** icon in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.ZoneList.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column, and select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.util.component.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>** .

   A dialog box appears, as shown in the following figure. 

   ![1](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/0014306461/p394682.png)

3. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.CreateTagDrawer.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>** .
