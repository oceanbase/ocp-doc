Update service configurations
==================================================

This topic describes how to update the configurations of an existing service.

Prerequisite
---------------------------------

* Make sure that you have the following permissions:

  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Host Maintenance** permission
  * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Subscriptions.TargetLayout.Service">Service</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.BackupStrategy.Edit.BackupAndRestoration">Backup & Recovery</UI-TERM>**

* You have installed a backup or recovery service. For more information, see [Install a service](../1000.manage-backup-and-recovery-service/200.installation-services.md).

Procedure
------------------------------

1. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Backup \& Recovery** **\>** **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Service.Service">Service</UI-TERM>** .

2. Find the service to update. Click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Component.MachineResource.SeeMore">More</UI-TERM>** icon in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Tags.TagList.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column, and then select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.backup.UpdateConfigurations">Update Configurations</UI-TERM>** to go to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.InstallService.UpdateConfigurations">Update Configurations</UI-TERM>** page.

   You can modify the storage configuration, backup configuration, and recovery configuration. For more information, see [Install a service](../1000.manage-backup-and-recovery-service/200.installation-services.md).

3. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Backup.Service.Edit.UpdateConfigurations">Update Configurations</UI-TERM>** .
