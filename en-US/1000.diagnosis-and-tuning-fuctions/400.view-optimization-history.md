# View the Optimization Center page

This topic describes how to use the features on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Optimize.OptimizationCenter">Optimization Center</UI-TERM>** page.

<main id="notice" type='notice'>
  <h4>Applicability</h4>
  <p>This topic applies only to OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) Enterprise Edition. OCP Community Edition does not support the capacity center feature. </p>
</main>

## Prerequisites

Make sure that you have the following permissions:

* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.ResourcePermissions">Resource Permissions</UI-TERM>**: **Cluster Read-only** or **Tenant Read-only** permission
* **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.MenuPermissions">Menu Permissions</UI-TERM>**: Permission on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Optimize.OptimizationCenter">Optimization Center</UI-TERM>** menu of **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.hook.useMenu.AutonomousService">OceanBase Autonomy Service</UI-TERM>**

## Procedure

1. Log on to the OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, click **Autonomy Service**.

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Diagnosis.ClusterDetails">Cluster Details</UI-TERM>** section, click the name of the cluster that you want to view.

4. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Optimize.OptimizationCenter">Optimization Center</UI-TERM>**.

5. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Optimize.OptimizationSuggestions">Optimization Suggestions</UI-TERM>** tab, you can view the following information:

   1. At the top of the page, you can view the optimization information related to exception events in the last three days in fields such as **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.Object">Object</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.SQLDiagnosis.Component.Plan.Name">Name</UI-TERM>**, **Behavior**, **Current Status**, **Occurrence Time**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.CardAbnormalTable.RecoveryTime">Recovery Time</UI-TERM>**, and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.SQLDiagnosis.TimeRange">Duration</UI-TERM>**.

   2. You can select one tenant or all tenants in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Resource.Component.Block.Tenant">Tenants</UI-TERM>** section to view the charts of information, such as the QPS, response time, number of sessions, and CPU utilization of the tenants.

      * **QPS**: the average number of SQL statements processed per second.
      * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.monitor.ResponseTime">Response Time</UI-TERM>**: the average processing time of an SQL statement.
      * **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.monitor.Sessions">Sessions</UI-TERM>**: the number of tenant sessions.
      * **Tenant CPU consumption**: the percentage of the number of CPU cores occupied by the tenant to the number of CPU cores allocated to the tenant.

      You can move the pointer over a curve to view the information at that point in time, and click the zoom-in button in the upper right corner of a chart to zoom in the chart.

   3. In the upper-right corner of the section, you can select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.RangeTimeDropdown.LastMinutes">Last 5 Minutes</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.component.OCPRangePicker.constant.NearlyMinutes.3">Last 30 Minutes</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Component.PerformanceTop5.LastHour">Last 1 Hour</UI-TERM>**, or **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.log.NearlyHours.1">Last 6 Hours</UI-TERM>**, or specify a custom period to view statistics within the specified period.

   4. In the section below the charts, you can view the optimization records of SQL statements and schemas in columns such as **Optimization Type**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Alarm.Event.Tenant">Tenant</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.ComparisonSqlDrawer.DetailedTable.Database">Database</UI-TERM>**, **Optimization Objects**, **Reasons for Diagnosis**, and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Component.SQLDetail.SQLTunning.OptimizationSuggestions">Optimization Suggestions</UI-TERM>**.

   5. You can click **View SQL Diagnostics** to view real-time diagnostics information.

   ![1](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/ocp/401/%E4%BC%98%E5%8C%96%E8%AE%B0%E5%BD%95.png)

6. Click the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Optimize.OptimizedRecords">Optimization Records</UI-TERM>** tab.

   In the upper-right corner, specify a time range to view the corresponding optimization records in columns such as **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Optimize.OperationDescription">Operation Description</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-performance-report.ReportTables.CacheStatInfo.SizeInfoTable.TenantName">Tenant Name</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Binlog.New.DatabaseName">Database Name</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Auth.Role.AddRole.OperationType">Operation Type</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Optimize.OperationDetails">Operation Details</UI-TERM>**, **SQL ID**, **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Optimize.OperationTime">Operation Time</UI-TERM>**, and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Plan.Component.PlanExecuteTable.Status">Status</UI-TERM>**. You can filter the optimization records by the operation type and status.
